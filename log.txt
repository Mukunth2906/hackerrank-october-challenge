**ASSISTANT: DESIGN THESIS (SEANCE)**
Name: Stateful Evidence-And-Claim Engine. Thesis: Northstar failed by trusting claims over observations, so the system separates the two and proves absence.
1. **Claim vs Observation ledger.** Snapshot/file contents are observations (high trust). Activity-log "SUCCESS" and `reported_status` are claims (low trust). A ghost is a disagreement between the two.

3. **Resurrection graph.** Active flows and restore paths are edges. Report only flows that touch this user.

5. **Ghost-injection evaluation.** Take clean cases, inject one ghost of a known type, and assert the status flips and the step count is correct. Report recall per ghost type.
6. **Uncertainty-routed LLM.** Deterministic code first. The LLM/VLM is called only for residual uncertainty, with schema-constrained output, double-read consensus and file-hash caching.
7. **Reverse-engineer generator conventions** from `sample_output.csv` into config, not per-case answers.
